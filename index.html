OMP Personal Gmail

A personal desktop connection between Gmail, the Google Workspace CLI and the OMP assistant. It is operated by the account owner for their own mailbox, not offered as a public email service.

What it does

  • Search and read relevant email threads when the owner asks.
  • Extract requested details into the owner's local notes.
  • Prepare email drafts and apply requested stars or labels.
  • Send an email only after the owner explicitly approves the final draft.

The requested Gmail permission also technically permits composing, sending and modifying email. The approval requirement is a workflow rule, not a narrower OAuth permission.

How data is handled

The assistant runs from the owner's computer. Selected email content may be sent to the AI providers configured by the owner to perform the requested task, and may appear in local assistant history or notes. This public website contains only application information: it has no mailbox connection, sign-in form, email data or OAuth credentials.

Read the privacy policy before authorising access. Access can be revoked through Google Account connections.

Ownership and contact

Maintained by the owner of the OMP Personal Gmail repository. For application or privacy questions, open an issue without including private messages, booking details, passwords or tokens.

This personal integration is not an official Google product. OMP and the Google Workspace CLI are separate software projects; this page does not claim their endorsement.
